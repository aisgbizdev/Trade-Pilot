---
name: Indonesian trader copy
description: Product-language guidance for natural Indonesian trading terminology.
---

Use clear, conversational Indonesian for explanations and actions, but retain English trading terms that Indonesian traders commonly use, such as entry, setup, timeframe, pullback, breakout, risk budget, Stop Loss, and Take Profit. Avoid literal translations that sound bureaucratic or unfamiliar.

**Why:** The user confirmed that direct English-to-Indonesian translation makes the product sound too formal and unlike language used by actual traders.

**How to apply:** When writing or revising Indonesian UI copy, prioritize trader familiarity and speed of comprehension over complete localization. Translate the surrounding sentence naturally while keeping established trading vocabulary intact.
