---
name: Symbol-scoped news feeds
description: Why per-symbol RSS must still pass instrument-market relevance checks
---

Treat a provider's symbol-scoped feed as a candidate pool, not proof of relevance. Broad terms (dollar, energy, SEC, spot ETF) and corporate coverage that only mentions a commodity or currency in passing should not qualify an article for an analysis.

**Why:** Feed labels and general keyword matches can admit company stories unrelated to the price or market for the selected instrument. A generic ETF headline may not even identify which asset the fund tracks.

**How to apply:** When changing instrument news selection, require a specific asset or directly applicable macro catalyst and an actual market-facing context. Keep sparse results sparse instead of backfilling with ambiguous stories; leave the global ticker's broader selection independent.
